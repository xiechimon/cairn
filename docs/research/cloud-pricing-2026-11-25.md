## 0. The Newcomer, Why, and User Scenarios

### 0.1 The Newcomer (Day 1)
Imagine a developer who installs Cairn today and has never heard of Firecracker microVMs, SQLite WAL, or Runner protocols:
- **Where they meet Cairn Cloud & why it exists for them:** On Day 1, Cairn launches straight into a free local workspace on their own computer — no account, no credit card, every feature unlocked (`Apache-2.0`). They encounter **Cairn Cloud** when they close their laptop and want scheduled routines, Telegram/Slack/WeChat bots, phone access, or unattended coding tasks to keep running without buying a 24/7 home server or managing VPS security, TLS, and backups.
- **What they must already know:** Zero infrastructure knowledge. One simple mental model: **Cairn Cloud = one always-available personal cloud computer.** Their Cairn brain and fallback coding workspace live there; they can still connect any number of their own Mac, Linux, Windows, or server machines for free whenever those machines are online.
- **What accounts, payments, and keys it asks of them:**
  - *Free (Self-Hosted):* No Cairn account, $0 forever, bring your own AI subscription or API key.
  - *Cairn Cloud (Plus / Pro):* One **Clerk** sign-in (Google, GitHub, or email), card / Apple Pay / Google Pay checkout via **Creem** (Merchant of Record, tax added at checkout where applicable; monthly or 10-for-12 annual), and **your own AI subscription or API key** (Cairn never bills or marks up AI tokens).
- **Where their data is stored:** In an isolated encrypted volume dedicated to their account (never shared across users), with automated daily encrypted backups to object storage (Cloudflare R2). Full `CAIRN_HOME` export back to their own computer is free at any time. After cancellation, the cloud computer and data are kept for **30 days** (with a 7-day warning email) before deletion.
- **Where they get stuck & what they see on failure:**
  - *No AI key/subscription configured yet:* Guided banner prompting them to connect an API key or sign in on an online machine; never a cryptic error.
  - *Cloud computer waking from sleep for a coding task:* Status badge shows *"Waking your cloud computer (~1–2s)..."* while the always-on Brain responds to chat immediately.
  - *Task hits wall-clock or runaway loop limit:* Task pauses cleanly with a clear explanation (e.g., *"Paused after 60 min — click Resume to continue"*) instead of silently burning compute or locking up the workspace.
  - *Cloud unreachable:* Desktop/web shows an offline read-only cache banner and never locks out local export or switching back to local mode.

### 0.2 Why This Product Shape (Locked Owner Decisions + Market Signal)
1. **No free cloud tier (`owner decision`):** Free = self-hosting open-source Cairn (`Apache-2.0`). Eliminating a free cloud tier also removes >95% of automated crypto-mining and account-farming abuse (`实测` Fly.io & Replit lessons, §8).
2. **One simple product per tier — "one always-available personal cloud computer" (`owner decision`, modeled on `dusk.bot/pricing`):** No hourly metering, no overage anxiety, no connected-machine caps (unlimited user machines on every tier), no bundled AI token credits. Tiers differ **only** by the cloud computer's compute/storage spec and price.
3. **Why BYO AI + flat $20 / $50 beats a $100+ token bundle (`实测`):**
   - **a16z Top 100 Gen AI Apps (7th Ed., Oct 2026, YipitData credit-card panel):** Median AI subscriber spends **$25/mo**, and **only 13%** of AI subscribers pay for a second AI product ([`a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps-7`](https://a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps-7/), `实测`).
   - **Simon Willison (Jul 2025):** Engineers can justify *one* $200/mo model plan, not two ([`simonwillison.net`](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Jul/5/cursor-clarifying-our-pricing/), `实测`).
   - **Menlo Ventures (Sep 2026, n=5,067):** **92%** of AI Agent users pay for AI and **63%** are daily active ([`menlovc.com`](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2026-the-state-of-consumer-ai/), `实测`).
   - **ChartMogul AI Churn Wave (Dec 2025, n=3,500 SaaS):** AI-native SaaS under `$50/mo` suffers **32% annualized NRR** (23% GRR), while `$50–$249/mo` doubles retention to **61% NRR** (45% GRR), and annual billing lifts NRR by **+10–20 percentage points** ([`chartmogul.com`](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-retention-the-ai-churn-wave/), `实测`).
   - **Takeaway (`推断`):** Anchoring Plus at **$20/mo** sits inside the $20–$25 consumer comfort zone alongside users' existing Claude/ChatGPT subscription, while **Pro at $50/mo** crosses ChartMogul's $50 retention cliff and captures the heavy coding segment (top 10% of AI spenders generate ~50% of category spend, `实测` a16z).

> **Why no "connected machine limit" or "coding hours" rows on the pricing page:** Per owner decision, connecting any number of your own Mac/Linux/Windows/server Runners is unlimited and free on every tier, and both the Brain and fallback coding execute on your personal cloud computer. > **Single-Vendor Fly.io Fallback Sensitivity (`推断`, §5.4):** The `$20 / $50` price list requires **InstaBox** (auto-pause Firecracker at `$0.017/$0.034/hr` and `$0.08/GB-mo`) for the Coding VM. If Cairn Cloud stays **100% on Fly.io** without InstaBox, Plus blended margin at `$20/mo` drops to **32.6%** (below the 45% target); under pure Fly.io, Plus must be priced at **`$25/mo` (`$250/yr`)** to reach **45.1%** blended margin (Pro stays `$50/mo` at **50.4%** margin).

## 4. Security Isolation & Sleep/Wake Architecture for "One Cloud Computer"

### 4.1 Codebase Audit: Why Naive Co-Location Fails Immediately (`实测`)
Inspecting this worktree (`e1e429810`) exposes **four critical risks** if Cairn Brain and Coding tasks share the same VM and OS user:
1. **Coding tasks currently run completely unsandboxed (`实测`):** [`apps/daemon/src/backend/claude-code.ts:1441-1456`](../../apps/daemon/src/backend/claude-code.ts#L1441-L1456) spawns the harness directly via `spawn(command, args, { env, detached: true })`, and [`apps/daemon/src/backend/pi.ts:96`](../../apps/daemon/src/backend/pi.ts#L96) executes built-in shell tools directly. The `apps/daemon/src/orb/` directory referenced in `spec 18 §1.1` no longer exists in this tree.
2. **Linux `bwrap` in `host-shell-confinement.ts` is NOT used for coding tasks and has two major gaps (`实测`):** [`apps/daemon/src/backend/host-shell-confinement.ts:345-368`](../../apps/daemon/src/backend/host-shell-confinement.ts#L345-L368) only covers `HostComputerProvider`, uses `--bind / /` (blacklist, not allowlist), **omits `--unshare-pid`** (allowing `/proc/<brain-pid>/environ` and `/proc/<brain-pid>/mem` reads under same UID), and **omits `--unshare-net`** (completely ignoring `options.serverPort` on Linux, leaving `127.0.0.1:8787` and cloud IMDS `169.254.169.254` wide open).
3. **`secretbox.key` sits `0o600` on disk in `CAIRN_HOME` (`实测`):** [`apps/server/src/lib/secret-box.ts:55-69`](../../apps/server/src/lib/secret-box.ts#L55-L69) writes the raw 32-byte AES-256-GCM key to `secretbox.key` (`0o600`) alongside `server.db` (`config.ts:251-256`), all owned by `USER node` in [`Dockerfile:26-34`](../../Dockerfile#L26-L34). Any malicious `npm postinstall` / `pip setup.py` / prompt-injected bash command running as `node` can steal `secretbox.key` + `server.db` and decrypt every stored API key, GitHub token, OAuth token, and IM bot secret offline.
4. **Brain cannot sleep (`实测`):** [`apps/server/src/services/scheduler.ts:73-120`](../../apps/server/src/services/scheduler.ts#L73-L120) runs an in-process 15-second tick loop (cron, PR watch, CI poller), and [`apps/server/src/services/im/`](../../apps/server/src/services/im/) maintains active outbound connections for **all three IM channels**: Telegram `getUpdates` long-polling (`telegram.ts:331`), Slack Socket Mode WebSocket (`slack.ts:510`), and WeChat iLink `getupdates` long-polling (`wechat.ts:328`). Putting `apps/server` inside a sleeping VM breaks all schedules and IM bots unless you build a Dusk-style central `Wake for Slack` proxy that custodies users' IM tokens ([`dusk.bot/terms`](https://dusk.bot/terms), `实测`).

## 6. Payment (Creem MoR) & Auth (Clerk) Cost Analysis

### 6.1 Creem Merchant of Record (`实测` from `creem.io/pricing`, `docs.creem.io`)
- **Base fee:** **`3.9% + $0.40 USD`** per successful transaction, `$0/mo` platform fee, `$0` international card surcharge ([`creem.io/pricing`](https://www.creem.io/pricing)). Currencies: `USD` and `EUR` only.
- **Tax-on-fee trap (`实测`, [`payouts.md`](https://docs.creem.io/merchant-of-record/finance/payouts.md)):** Creem calculates `3.9% + $0.40` on **gross checkout total including VAT/GST/sales tax**. On a `$20` plan sold in France (20% VAT = `$24` gross), Creem charges `$1.33–$1.34` instead of `$1.18` (+0.78 pp). **Always configure `tax_mode: "exclusive"`** (tax added at checkout, matching `dusk.bot`'s `+ tax where applicable`).
- **Payout fees & China rules (`实测`, [`payouts.md`](https://docs.creem.io/merchant-of-record/finance/payouts.md), [`supported-countries.md`](https://docs.creem.io/merchant-of-record/supported-countries.md)):**
  - Bank transfer (via Wise): **`max($7 USD/EUR, 1%)`** (+ partner FX if local currency ≠ USD/EUR). Batch withdrawals ≥`$700` to hit the 1% floor. Polygon USDC: **2%**.
  - **Mainland China:** Individual KYC → **Alipay** (max `50,000 CNY/payout`, `300,000–600,000 CNY/yr`); Business KYB → **Local Bank Account (unlimited)**. Payouts run on the **1st & 15th** ($50 min, 7–12 day clearing hold).
  - **Buyer methods (`payment-methods.md`):** Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay (global recurring); **Alipay buyer checkout is one-time only (not recurring subscriptions)** — another strong reason to promote **annual 1-time/yearly billing** (`$200`/`$500`)!
- **Refunds & chargebacks (`实测`, [`refunds-and-chargebacks.md`](https://docs.creem.io/merchant-of-record/finance/refunds-and-chargebacks.md)):** Refunds carry `$0` extra fee, **but the original `3.9% + $0.40` is NOT refunded**. Chargebacks deduct **the full sale amount + `$25 USD/EUR` dispute fee** from merchant balance (Creem does NOT indemnify lost disputes). Creem may issue discretionary refunds within 60 days to avoid disputes.
- **Why flat tiers beat hourly micro-overages on Creem (`推断`):** A `$1` overage charge loses **44.5%** to Creem + payout fees; a `$2` charge loses **24.7%**. Dropping hourly overage billing eliminates both micro-fee leakage and Creem's unshipped self-serve auto-refill portal gap ([`auto-refill.md`](https://docs.creem.io/features/customer-credits/auto-refill.md), `实测`).
- **AI Moderation API exemption (`实测`, [`faq.md`](https://docs.creem.io/guides/faq.md)):** Creem's `$0.0003/1k chars` Moderation API mandate applies *only* to AI image/video generation — text/code products like Cairn Cloud are explicitly exempt.

## 7. Willingness to Pay, Churn & Price-Point Lessons (`实测`)

1. **The `$20–$25` Anchor & Subscription Stacking Wall:** Across 16 competitors, `$20/mo` is the universal personal entry point (Dusk Plus, Hermes Plus, Letta Pro, Devin Pro, Replit Core, Amp Megawatt, ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro). With median AI consumer spend at `$25/mo` and only 13% paying for a second AI tool (`a16z`), Cairn Cloud MUST position as **"your $20 always-on cloud computer for the AI you already pay for"** — not a competing LLM subscription.
2. **The `$50` Retention Cliff & Power-Law Expansion:** ChartMogul's 3,500-company study (`实测`) shows `<$50/mo` AI-native SaaS has brutal churn (**32% NRR**, because users treat Month 1 as a paid trial when there is no free cloud tier), while `$50–$249/mo` achieves **61% NRR**. Having a clear **Pro `$50/mo` (4 vCPU / 8 GB / 60 GB)** tier directly captures heavy coding users who outgrow 4 GB RAM and lifts fleet retention & profit.
3. **Annual Billing & Month-2 Conversion Lever:** Annual plans raise NRR by **+10–20 percentage points** and cut ARR churn by ~25% (`ChartMogul` & `Paddle`, `实测`). Crucially, **Month 2** has **3× higher monthly-to-annual conversion** than Month 9 (`实测`). Cairn Cloud should display `Pay 10 months, get 12` prominently at checkout and trigger an in-app "Switch to Annual & Save 2 Months" prompt during Month 2 of active usage.
4. **Why Flat Specs Beat Opaque Credits / Surprise Overages:** Cursor's June 2025 surprise-overage backlash (requiring a CEO apology & refunds), Replit's `$50/hr` runaway-loop Reddit outrage, and Manus/Bolt/Lovable credit-burn complaints (`实测`) prove developers deeply resent opaque credit burn during agent debugging loops. Flat `$20` / `$50` cloud computers completely eliminate billing anxiety.
