|---|---|---|
| [GitHub Actions workflow syntax](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/workflows-and-actions/workflow-syntax#concurrency), [reusing workflows](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/how-tos/reuse-automations/reuse-workflows) | Observed: job concurrency can protect a reusable workflow call; pending jobs are replaced by default, or queued with `queue: max`. | [Mergify parallel checks](https://docs.mergify.com/merge-queue/parallel-checks/) | Observed: checks on upcoming queue entries begin while earlier entries are being checked. | Inference: similarly overlap independent builds; do not give speculative work authority to update the published channel. |

Do not claim a second parallelization win for dependencies already parallel, or remove a verifier. |
