## Pattern that fits Cairn

1. **Single harness, no separate verifier agent** (**Inferred** from Cursor/Devin single-environment self-verification + Cairn's retired `verify` step history): Keep all implementation, live/deployed checks, and proof attachment inside the task's `session` step.
2. **Evidence-gated `done`, not advisory demos** (**Measured** from `docs/research/agent-regression-prevention-2026-11-26.md` "Evidence theater"): A change task cannot enter `done` solely on prose or local unit tests; `evaluateStepAcceptance` must require a fresh, non-empty (`sizeBytes > 0`), post-edit `attach_file` proof artifact (screenshot, request/response, DB row, or verified run output) created by that step/build.
3. **One-line missing visibility without schema bloat** (**Inferred** from Cairn's existing `TodoRecord.missingCommand` pattern in `apps/server/src/services/todos.ts`): Surface a concise single-line `missingEvidence` field derived from pending `acceptance-blocked-<stepId>` cards or active acceptance-nudge steps, and expose it on task rows and thread headers.
4. **Reuse stall-monitor (#19) plumbing** (**Measured** from `apps/server/src/services/stall-monitor.ts` and `step-permission-wait.ts`): Acceptance-retry turns share `sweepStallMonitor` recovery and `isStepWaitingPermission` wait protection; stall alerts during an acceptance retry include the missing-evidence line so stalled verification turns stay diagnosable.

## Concrete recommendation

- **`packages/shared/src/records/acceptance.ts`**: Require fresh, non-empty (`sizeBytes > 0`) post-edit step-scoped attachments across UI, API/server, CLI/daemon/script, doc, and other code edits; add `missingLine` on failed evaluations and `parseMissingEvidenceLine` helper.
- **`packages/shared/src/records/prompts.ts` & `apps/daemon/src/step-prompt.ts`**: Update `SESSION_BRIEF`, `SESSION_REWORK_BRIEF`, and `composeWorkerSystemPrompt` (archiving old strings in `LEGACY_*`) to fold production/deployed-target checks and proof attachment into the same `session` harness.
- **`packages/shared/src/records/todo.ts` & `apps/server/src/services/`**: Add `missingEvidence` to `TodoRecord`, persist `build.reportLine` on `askAcceptanceBlockedOnce`, derive `missingEvidence` in `todos.ts`, enrich stall-monitor notes in `stall-monitor.ts`, and select `attachment.sizeBytes` in `build-step-complete.ts`.
- **`apps/web/src/`**: Display the one-line missing reason in `tasks-sections.ts`, `tasks-panel.tsx`, `side-chat.tsx`, `mappers.ts`, and `i18n/en.ts`.
