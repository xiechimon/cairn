## Attack paths traced and why each is closed

1. **Dot-segment rewrite of api.github.com requests with the team token (the issue this PR fixes).**
   - Before: `../user` passed `/^[A-Za-z0-9._-]+\/[A-Za-z0-9._-]+$/`. `encodeURIComponent('..')` is `..`, and fetch normalizes `https://api.github.com/repos/../user/issues` to `/user/issues`. Node confirmed: `..`/`x` gives `/x/issues`.
   - Now: `parseGithubRepoRef` (packages/shared/src/records/project.ts:69) rejects `.` and `..` for either segment. `isGithubRepoRef` (:80) is its boolean face.
   - Bypass variants checked:
     - `%2e`/`%2E` forms: `%` is outside the character class.
     - Backslash: outside the class.
     - Trailing newline: JS `$` without the m flag matches only at end of input. Node: `"a/b\n"` gives null.
     - Extra slash (`a/b/c`): rejected.
     - `...`, `.....`, `.git`: allowed, and they stay literal segments. Node: `/repos/.../x/issues`.
   - Closed.

2. **New writes of a dot-segment githubRepo.** Every writer of project.githubRepo validates through isGithubRepoRef:
   - POST /api/projects (routes-projects.ts:205-213). The object form `{owner, repo}` is joined and then validated, so `{owner:"..",repo:"x"}` gets a 400.
   - PATCH local-to-github conversion (services/projects.ts:184). This runs before the `githubRepoReachable` fetch.
   - chief `connect_repo` (chief-tools.ts:601).

   The only other write sets null (chief-tools.ts:578). Closed.
