daemon(1744): preserve thinking budgets during summary recovery (#1933)

Retry message-shaped compaction summaries that exceed provider context limits.
Preserve enabled thinking budgets and reserve at least one additional output
token; shorten conversation input when output cannot fit that constraint.
Keep ordinary requests and non-ceiling errors unchanged, and retain bounded
retry logs and explicit terminal errors.

Verified with 22 compaction cases, including test-first failures for both
reported-input and generic ceiling errors, plus captured retry parameters.
Port the original lessons to main's per-file ledger without restoring its
obsolete table.

Part of #1744. Live production step survival and Responses-shaped requests
remain outside this delivery's verified scope.
