For a current deployment, use [Getting started](../getting-started.md) or the [self-hosted VPS guide](../deploy/self-hosted-vps.md). For private phone access, follow [Use Cairn on your devices over Tailscale](../deploy/tailscale-mobile.md). For a database move or recovery, follow [Server backup and recovery](../ops-db-backup.md).
