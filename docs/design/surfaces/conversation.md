# Coordinator conversation and home

The `/app` home: the Coordinator's conversation, its composer, status mark, channels and the right-hand panels' shared rules. Reference shots: `docs/design/references/coordinator-chat/` (Claude.ai for replies and the composer, Claude Duet for the shell, floating mark, task references and panels). Everything the Coordinator says sits in a grey reply bubble, its one-line reports included: `已停止，没有建任务。` after a stopped turn, `已转给任务 …` and the `已建任务 …` fallback are `--reply-bubble-fill` bubbles in `text-ui` ink, like the task activity rows. Notes that are not the Coordinator speaking (who started a merge, which machine a turn ran on) stay status lines in `text-meta` `--text-tertiary`.

There is no floating Coordinator launcher or window: the Coordinator lives only on the home route `/app`. The sidebar's `Coordinator` row is the entry; Cmd/Ctrl+J, notification links and question links navigate to the home, and pending questions raise the row's needs-you count. `apps/web/test/no-floating-coordinator.test.ts` fails if a floating Coordinator returns.

## Shell and top bar

The Coordinator home has a 48px top bar with no visible title (the `Coordinator` or project `h1` is screen-reader only) and the `任务`, `成果` and computer icon buttons on the right; the floating status mark sits top centre.

The status mark (`chief/chief-status-mark.tsx`) is the Coordinator's mascot written straight on the page, with no disc or shadow: one brush line (a filled outline that lands heavy and lifts thin, never textured) at 40px, `foreground` ink, rust for the error cross, 55% ink while offline. Each of the ten states of `statusMarkView` is its own gesture, the brand spiral when idle; a change erases the old line along its path (250ms) and draws the new one (350ms). Idle, done and error hold still; working states re-ink at 5 frames a second; the question bubble carries no dot. The state word sits under it in `text-meta` `--text-secondary` whenever the mark is not idle and changes with the state (something is happening, so there is a word); idle has none, and done's word leaves with done after 2.2 seconds. Hovering adds the turn's elapsed time; the phone shows no word. On the empty home, where the large spiral already stands, the idle mark is hidden; with no model source the empty home shows neither, only the centred connect-a-model-source prompt (a `text-page` lead, a 44px primary button, a `text-ui` hint). At 16 to 24px (the sidebar `Coordinator` row, `CoordinatorGlyph`) the states fold to the brand spiral: tracing while working, the 6px `--waiting-ink` dot while a question waits, an ink cross after an error, dimmed while offline.

The computer button, also the round one on the phone, carries no status dot of its own as in the reference: the Coordinator machine's state is in its tooltip and accessible name, and the fleet badge at the icon's corner carries the machines' online state (see `docs/design/surfaces/computers.md`); it dims only while the connection is down.

The `任务` and `成果` buttons open the right-hand `SidePanel`. `任务` is one list grouped as needs you, in progress and idle (schedules inside idle, shown once and only while their task is not active); `成果` lists finished documents such as research reports, never code changes, which the Tasks panel already shows with their PRs; a card is the title, the report's opening paragraph and its age, and opens the report as a document with its kind, stage and time above a serif title.

Nothing sits above the composer except one notice at a time, in this order: connection lost, `Coordinator 暂时不可用`, the queue-wait card, the failed-turn card, the fallback-model note. The queue-wait card is the same card with an ochre icon for a turn waiting on a computer that is not ready or an offline Runner; it leaves on its own when the turn runs and writes nothing to the conversation. The failed-turn card shows only for the newest failure while no owner message follows it: the outage banner's surface with a rust `CircleAlert`, the title, one sentence, the kind's actions (the first `default`, the rest `outline`), `详情` with the raw error, and a close button; close or Escape hides it, and retry or the next message removes it.

## Coordinator channels

Design `docs/design/coordinator-project-channels.md`. The main channel (`/app`) and each project channel (`/app?project=<id>&chat=1`) are separate Coordinator conversations, each with its own thread, session and turn queue; switching channels switches the thread, never filters one transcript. A channel shows only its own messages; the Coordinator reads another channel with the read-only `channel_messages` tool. Task reports return to the channel that created the task (`create_todo` makes that channel watch it), and a project-channel task that needs the owner or is done also leaves one line in the main channel, carrying the channel it came from.

- **First open of a project channel** creates its thread and one greeting turn: the usual typing state, then a Coordinator bubble of one to three sentences that names the owner, mentions anything in the project waiting on them and asks what to do, with two or three reply chips for that project, from `suggest_replies` during the greeting or from the request that follows it. It runs once per thread. If the owner sends first, the greeting is dropped.
- **Fixed greeting**: when there is nothing to show, the conversation opens with a Coordinator bubble instead of a heading: `{时段}好，{name}。你想让我做什么？` in the main channel, `{name}，你想在 {project} 里做什么？` in a project channel, each with three reply chips `做个计划` / `接一个仓库` / `接着最近的活`. A project channel shows it only when its greeting turn never ran or failed without recovery eligibility, never while the turn's reply is still arriving. A retryable failed initial greeting instead keeps its error visible; only the latest failure offers explicit retry, with the shared plain-language reason and model settings link. Retrying copies the saved greeting prompt without inventing an owner message. No serif heading, date line or counts sit above any conversation; the waiting count is the `任务` badge.
- **Archived project channel**: the transcript as it was, no greeting bubble and no chips.

## Messages and bubbles

- User turns sit on the right in a flat warm sand bubble (`UserMessageBubble`, `data-slot="user-bubble"`): one `--bubble-fill`, no border, no texture and no gradient, `--radius-bubble` (18px) corners and a tail at the bottom right (an 18px-tall flare that leaves the right edge and ends 6px past it on the bubble's baseline, drawn as a clipped `::after` in the bubble's own fill, measured on the reference recording at 1:52), `8px 14px` padding (36px for one line, measured on the reference recording), at most `min(85%, 42rem)` wide.
- Conversation text (user bubble, Coordinator reply, composer) is `text-ui` at a 20px line (`CHAT_BODY_CLS`, 14/20), and the Coordinator column, composer included, is 756px (`max-w-chat`), both measured on the reference recording.
- Agent and Coordinator replies carry no avatar column. One reply is one grey bubble: all of a reply's paragraphs, headings, blockquote and lists sit inside a single `--reply-bubble-fill` bubble sized to its content, at most `--container-reply-bubble` (512px) wide, `--radius-reply` (16px) corners, `7px 14px` padding (34px for one line, measured on the reference recording); blocks that were separated by a blank line keep a 10px gap inside the bubble and tighter runs keep 4px. Consecutive messages from one author with no divider or other row between them cluster the same way (`chief/bubble-runs.ts`): 4px apart instead of 20px, on touch too, and only the last owner bubble of a run carries the tail. Tables and code blocks are not bubbled: white surfaces with a 1px `--border` (tables 8px corners, a `secondary` header row, 12px cell padding; code blocks `--radius-code` 10px, a 40px header over a 1px rule: the language label at 12px in `ink-2`, 20px from the left edge, and the expand and copy buttons, 32px at a 32px pitch, each a 16px glyph in full `ink`; measured on the reference, where the glyphs draw 12 to 14.5px of ink in near-black and the label reads #565656). Links in chat text are `--chat-link` (the reference blue, AA on the bubble fills) with a 40% underline in the same hue. Markdown goes through `marked` with GFM and the CJK emphasis extension (`lib/markdown.ts`, the only module that imports it); raw HTML stays literal text and links other than http(s) and cairn's own mention and attachment targets stay literal. `- [ ]`/`- [x]` are read-only checkboxes drawn as cairn's todo glyphs: a 16px dashed `--text-tertiary` circle when open, a filled `--text-tertiary` circle with a check when done, with the done text struck through in `--text-tertiary`; the agent todo card is only for a list carrying the agent's `[~]`/`[/]` progress markers. Headings: H1 18px, H2 and H3 15px, deeper levels body size, all semibold. A reply from a bound agent (not the coordinator) keeps a small identity line above it. Hovering or focusing a bubble shows a white pill toolbar beside it, 12px away, vertically centred: right of a reply bubble (a direct `复制` icon button, then the time and any fallback-model note in `--text-tertiary`; no reply arrow and no `…` menu), left of an owner bubble (the time, then `回复`, `复制`, `…` holding `恢复到此处`). A `…` menu appears only when the message has an action besides copy. When a wide bubble leaves no room beside it inside the conversation (a narrow window with a mouse), the toolbar shifts inward to stay 8px inside the visible column and its time gets a popover backing so it stays legible over the bubble. `…` holds `复制` (a `已复制` toast) and, for owner messages, `恢复到此处`; there is no reaction button. On touch screens a tap on a bubble reveals that message's tool row under it (`data-tools-open`, `chief/touch-message-tools.ts`). Replying starts only from a message's `回复` tool; selecting text shows no reply bar. While the Replying to bar is hovered, the quoted bubble darkens one fill step (`--reply-bubble-highlight` for a reply, `--bubble-highlight` for an owner bubble, no ring), and the mark clears as soon as the quote is removed or sent.
- **No agent label on a message** (C16; there are no dedicated agents): no agent chip, avatar dot or agent link in a reply, a notice, a user bubble or a task transcript. An agent mention kept in an old message reads as its name in plain text, and the mention picker offers no agents.
- **Historical daily reports render read-only** (C17): existing Coordinator bubbles keep the sentence with yesterday's done, merged and failed counts and the file-icon line that opens the report as a document in the research-report dialog; no buttons and no interactive suggestion states — the suggestion decision surface and the `chief_report`/`retro_proposal` tables are retired, and no new reports are generated.
- Markers between turns ("Plan approved", handoffs, origin notes) are a centred hairline rule in `--text-tertiary`; a failed turn is the same kind of centred hairline marker, whatever failed and however old: a 16px `CircleAlert` and `没完成：<title>` in `--text-tertiary` `text-meta` with a chevron; clicking it opens the plain sentence and the raw error with a copy button, and it carries no button. The title and sentence come from `turnErrorCopy` over `turnErrorKind` (`docs/design/coordinator-turn-failure.md`). A turn longer than a minute ends with a "Worked for N minutes" rule.
- Copy icons under a message stay hidden until its row is hovered or focused (`.chat-copy` in `app.css`); touch screens always show them.
- The plan preview fades out at the bottom with a mask instead of a hard cut.
- **File tiles** (`components/composer-attachment-tile.tsx`, data in `board/file-tile.tsx` and `board/file-data.ts`): a 122px tile per sent or composer file. An image fills it; a video shows its first frame under a round play badge; any other file shows its first lines (text, code, log, diff, Markdown, and the extracted text of docx, pptx and xlsx) at three-quarter scale fading out at the bottom, or, when there is nothing cheap to show (pdf, archives, audio, unknown), its type glyph centred. A footer carries a 16px type glyph and the name. Type glyphs come from Open Design (`vendor-open-design/file-type-icon.tsx`) with the conventional accent per office type (`--file-word`, `--file-excel`, `--file-ppt`, `--file-pdf`; owner decision 2026-11-20 over V5); other types keep the lucide file glyph in icon ink.
- **File lightbox** (`board/file-lightbox.tsx`, mounted once in `ChiefRoot`): clicking any tile or attachment chip opens a full-window viewer over the app, never a side panel. The window dims to `--lightbox-scrim`; the head holds the type glyph and name on the left and, on the right, source toggle (Markdown, SVG), copy (text types), download and close, all `icon-sm` ghost buttons in `--lightbox-ink`. Images, SVG and video sit on the scrim fitted to the window; every other type sits in a `--radius-card` sheet up to `max-w-4xl` wide. When the message holds more than one file, a pager `‹ 第 n 个，共 N 个 ›` is centred at the bottom and left and right keys step through the message's files. Esc, a click on the scrim and the close button close it; the URL carries `file` and `fileName`, so Back closes it too and a link opens it directly. Each type renders through the viewer borrowed from Open Design (`vendor-open-design/viewers/`, one file per viewer; the file-type table is in `docs/design/design-surface.md`): code and text with line numbers and highlighting, `.diff` and `.patch` with added and removed rows tinted, `.json` formatted, Markdown rendered by `ChatMarkdown` with a source toggle, images and SVG fitted, video and audio with native controls, PDF in the browser frame, `.docx`, `.pptx` and `.xlsx` as extracted text, and anything else, or a document the server cannot read, as a download state with the size. Text views stop at 5000 lines with a download note. The side panel is reserved for the design view.
- Nothing sits above the first loaded message of the Coordinator conversation: no caption says earlier messages exist, and scrolling to the top loads them (reference recording).
- **Scroll and tools** (C18, Claude.ai): after sending, the user's message and the start of the reply are in the viewport; when the user has scrolled away, a jump-to-latest control appears. Message tools show on hover or keyboard focus only, the latest message included, as a pill beside the hovered bubble (reference recording); on touch one row under the message appears only after a tap or long-press on the bubble, and a tap elsewhere hides it.

## Task references and chips

The reply whose turn created a task gets a reference row above its bubble (matching the iOS app; rows never nest: research is the task's own first step, a request split into research and code shows two sibling rows, and research opened by a stuck retry shows no row), tinted by the task's state (`TodoStatusTile` in `detail/segments.tsx`). Every mention of a task, in a reply, a notice, a user bubble or a task transcript, is a chip in the state's ink (`taskChipClass`). The chip is an outlined pill (a flat grey strip on a grey bubble had no edge): a `bg-popover` fill with the `--e1-lift` ring and soft drop. The reference row above the bubble stays unframed: no fill, ring or shadow. A 1.5px `--task-ref-line` elbow (round caps, one 6px quarter-circle) rises from 6px above the bubble, 18px in from its edge, to the tile's centre line, stopping 3px short of the tile; sibling rows share its trunk. The row is a 20px rounded-square tile (`--radius-sm`) holding a 12px `StatusGlyph`, then the title in `text-ui` semibold ink; no status word trails the title: the glyph shows the state and the row's accessible name and tooltip carry it in words. The chip is a pill on the 20px line (`leading-[inherit]`, `align-top`, no vertical padding, so a paragraph holding chips keeps its line height; the ring is a shadow, so it adds no height) with 6px side padding, 6px radius, a 12px glyph and the title in `text-ui` regular, in the state's ink. Tone per phase: planning and building = `tint-working` with the spinning ring; confirm, needs input and review follow the reference: an eye glyph in `--task-wait-ink` on a 16px `--task-wait-tint` (green) circle, the chip's title in `--chat-link`, the reference-row tile in `--task-wait-tint`; done, to start, queued, closed = `tint-quiet`; failed = `tint-fail`. Reduced motion stops the ring. A mention of a task the page does not know has no state and stays the neutral chip. The chip sits flush with the text edge when it starts a line; a title of up to 16 characters never breaks, a longer one wraps as one chip with open ends at the break; in dark mode an inactive chip's title uses the body ink on a fill lighter than the reply bubble.

The reference row is the only way a task appears in the conversation (the separate task card repeated it and is gone). A task the Coordinator created folds into the reply that announced it (`foldTaskCards`, `chief/chief-task-refs.ts`): one row per task in the whole conversation, a task the reply both mentions and created shows once, and the three-row cap on mentioned tasks never drops a created one. A task created while the turn is still running hangs on that turn's live row (the typing dots) from the moment it exists, so the tree never moves when the reply lands (Motion, Growth); a task created in a finished turn with no reply stands as the same row without the elbow (`chief-task-started`); a task the page no longer knows keeps the one-line `已建任务 #N` link. An unread task carries a 6px `--card-button` dot after its title. The status word, the age, the update count and the latest line are not printed in the conversation: they live in the task panel the row opens and in the Tasks panel row's tooltip.

**Naming a task** (C15): a task is named by its title wherever a person reads it — rows, chips, links, status and report lines, confirm dialogs and notifications. A linked mention written as `[#n](todo:<id>)` shows the title as a task chip in every renderer. A task the page does not know reads `任务`; a link whose label is not a bare `#n` keeps its author's words. Prose a model wrote as `#n` followed by that task's title (`#6 写作文`, `#6「写作文」`) or as `任务 #n` shows the same chip, the title swallowed into it; a bare `#n`, `PR #n` or `owner/repo#n` stays literal. The sequence number shows only in what the user types: the `#` mention picker and the `#n` it inserts.

## Composer

- The composer is a one-line raised field (`--composer-fill`: the page surface in light, the reply-bubble grey in dark, as in the reference) with a full 1px inset ring (`--composer-ring`, 13% black light / 7% white dark, not the half-pixel hairline) that strengthens on focus, a soft drop (`--composer-drop`, `0 1px 2px` + `0 3px 10px`), 14px corners and 46px tall at rest, all measured on the reference recording; the thread-panel composer and the narrow one-row composer share the tokens; under it sit the plain `+` menu (attachments, the thread target) and, at the right end, the current context usage: a 14px monochrome ring whose arc is the share of the model window in use, with no number beside it; a short tick on the ring marks the auto-compact point, and within a tenth of it the arc takes `foreground`. The reading in K with English terms (`Context 24K / 200K`, `70K 时 auto-compact` when the server sends a compaction point, `Cache 3 分钟后失效` when the provider guarantees a cache lifetime) is its tooltip and accessible name. The ring never spins: a running turn shows on the status mark and the stop button; while the usage fetch is in flight it is the still bare track. Clicking it opens the context panel (`chief/context-meter-ring.tsx`): `Context` and `76K / 200K`, a bar with the compact tick, then only the rows the provider supports: `Auto-compact`, `Cache` (an `m:ss 后失效` countdown, `已失效`, `回复中保持有效`, or `下一条不命中` after a model switch) and `Hit rate` (read over read, write and uncached input). Cache lifetime per provider: Anthropic 5 minutes or 1 hour, OpenAI 30 minutes from GPT-5.6 on, Claude Code by its recorded one-hour writes; Codex subscription, DeepSeek, Gemini and other providers show no Cache row. No manual compact control. It shows whenever context data exists, working or idle. The elapsed time of a running turn shows on hovering the floating status mark. The empty send button is a bare enter icon; once there is a draft the icon turns `foreground` and nothing fills. The stop button is a quiet square with an ink stop glyph.
- **Sending** (first slice of `docs/design/message-first-sending.md`): a message sent to the Coordinator shows at once as a user bubble at 55% opacity with no tools, the composer clears before the server answers, and the bubble turns solid on the answer. A failed send removes the bubble, returns the text to the composer after anything typed since, and shows the `发送失败，请重试。` toast; there is no failed row and nothing is persisted yet.
- **Coordinator spacing** (turn gap re-measured on the reference recording): 24px between a user turn and a reply either way (the reference measures 23 to 24px between bubbles), 20px before a status row or a time divider, on touch too (message tools there appear only on a tap), the status pill at 13px/20px and 34px tall (7px above and below the text, measured on the reference recording at 2:56) under the 44px disc, and `--composer-bottom-gap` 16px under the line below the composer (it was 24px; the task composer shares it).
- **Coordinator message furniture**: the hover toolbar beside a bubble carries its clock time (`11:20` today, the full date in the title); a gap of more than 10 minutes puts a centred caption divider (`今天 11:22`) above the next message; a reply whose turn created a task opens with a reference row above it (a small elbow, the status glyph and the title), and its sentences name any task as a chip. Quick-reply chips stay while the turn that offered them is still running (`docs/design/coordinator-quick-replies.md`).
- **Toasts** (reference recording A 1:21-1:30 and 2:48): on desktop every toast stacks at the bottom right, 16px from the right edge, and 112px from the bottom when a composer sits below it (the conversation, a thread panel), 16px when only a side panel is open; below 640px they keep the top-centre placement under the floating controls. A toast is one `popover` card: a 20px glyph in ink (`Info` for success and info; warning and error keep their glyphs and inks; green is never used, V5), the title in regular `text-ui`, the description in `text-meta`, a 28px ghost close button with an `X` at the right of the first line, and the action as a grey 28px `secondary` pill under the text.

## Trace

- The agent's narration stays body text. Tool activity folds into one summary row (`TraceGroupSummary`, `components/chat/trace-parts.tsx`, composed by `TraceGroup`, `TraceRows` and `WorkTrace`). The reference recording has no folded tool line, so the row borrows the rows of its step checklist card (12:28 to 12:56: a 16px state glyph, then the step in grey body type): 28px tall, sized to its content, 12px side padding so its glyph sits on the left edge of the card rows it opens, a 16px `StatusGlyph` in `--text-tertiary` (the filled check when the work is done, the spinning ring while it runs, the `--destructive` cross when a step failed), 8px, one line of `text-ui` 14/20 in `--text-secondary`, 8px, and a 16px `--text-tertiary` chevron that points right when closed and turns down when open, with no transition. Hover fills the whole row with `--menu-hover` at `--radius-control`; nothing is filled at rest, open or closed. The row is the same in the Coordinator conversation and the task thread. The finished line says what came of the group, not the steps it took (step names read as a log; the agent's narration above the group carries the purpose): the files it edited successfully (`改了 rate-limit.ts、login.ts`, first two names then `等 n 个文件`) with the verdict of the last finished test command when it ran after the last edit (`，测试通过` or `，测试未通过`), else that verdict alone, else the files it read (`读了 main.ts`), else the counts ("Ran 2 commands, changed 1 file"). While the group runs the line is the live purpose (the running command's description, or the last reasoning heading), else the present counts. The tooltip holds the purposes the agent gave (reasoning-summary headings and command descriptions, at most two) and the counts. Never hand-write tool rows. The live label (`LiveRow variant="chief"`) stays `text-meta` 13/20.
- Open, the group is a hairline card (1px inset `--border` ring, 12px radius from `--cds-radius-xl`) of 36px rows separated by hairlines. A row reads "verb **target**": the verb in `--text-tertiary`, the target in `--foreground` at medium weight, no kind icon and no code pill; commands are mono without a background. The duration, diff size or exit code sits at the row end in tabular numbers.
- A row with detail opens to a bordered command box (`$ command`, mono `text-code` 13/20, 8px radius, `--background`) with the output under it in grey mono `text-code`.
- Failures: the summary shows the `--destructive` cross and a `--destructive` count ("(1 failed)") and the row a `--destructive` "exit N" or "Failed" label; a failed command opens by default and prints its output in `--destructive`. Never use `--text-dim` for text.
- Running: the summary shows the spinning ring and switches to the present tense for the whole group ("Running 1 command", or the running command's purpose) and sweeps with the `shimmer` utility, as does the verb of the running row. Under reduced motion the shimmer stops.
- `components/ui/trace.tsx` keeps the low-level Trace primitives for thinking rows (`ThinkingRow`).

## Live run

- There is no separate live block: the open group is the live row. In the Coordinator conversation, the transcript ends in the typing bubble (`TypingDots`: three `typing-dot` marks in a filled pill, still under reduced motion); what the Coordinator is doing shows on the status mark, which also carries the queue and quota waits (`排队中`, `等额度`) that used to sit in a line above the composer, and the dots open nothing. A settled reply never lists its tool rows: the Coordinator conversation has no steps view, and calls on the owner's Mac leave no row at all (C26).
- When the turn ends the pulse disappears and the group turns past tense.
- **End space under the Coordinator conversation**: the scroller reaches down to the composer's top edge and reserves `--chat-end-space` (24px) below the last message, so a short conversation sits right above the quick-reply chips or the composer (the 160px Claude.ai measurement). The Coordinator stream has no gap above the composer: scrolled, text runs up under the floating header to the window's top edge (the list starts at the top of the pane and pads its first row below the 48px header) and down to the composer's own top edge, so no second line shows. Both edges carry a soft `scroll-fade` like the Claude Duet reference: 32px above the composer and 80px at the top where the list runs under the header or the narrow floating controls (32px below a banner), each off once the list rests at that end. Its scroller reserves the scrollbar gutter on both sides (`scrollbar-gutter-both`), so the message column and the composer share one centre and one width. `FadeScrollerViewport` requires an explicit `reserveEnd`. The task thread transcript reserves only `--chat-end-space`, running or idle, so the newest row sits right above the composer and a running task never leaves a blank band under its live line, as in Claude Code's own panel.

## Questions and approvals

- **Coordinator questions** (C11): the pending question sits at the end of the conversation as a reply bubble holding the question, then one outlined chip per option, identical to the suggested-reply chips (`REPLY_CHIP_CLS`: transparent fill, 1px `--border`, 28px pill, `text-ui` regular ink, 12px apart). Only labels show: the recommended option is the first chip and looks like the others at rest (no permanent fill or shadow; hover, focus and pressed states are shared); each chip's tooltip and accessible description carry `推荐 · ` and the description; a chip shows no number and digits never pick one. Nothing is drawn above the bubble: the question's header (the task name) is the bubble's tooltip and the question section's accessible description. Pending questions show one at a time, the oldest first; answering or dismissing brings the next. There is no pager in the conversation: the Tasks panel's `待你回答` rows and the needs-you badge focus a specific question (keyboard reach is through those rows; the old pager buttons and their tab stops are gone). Per-request drafts survive the switch, and while an answer's attachment uploads focus does not move: the row says `附件上传中` instead. A chip answers at once through the existing answer request (an early step of a multi-question request moves to the next step instead); a failure keeps the chips with the error line and toast. Multi-select chips toggle (pressed fill and a check) and an outlined `提交`/`下一步` chip submits. Digits are ordinary text and pick nothing; typing in the composer answers with free text (placeholder `输入你自己的回答…`, the send button submits). Escape on the chips, or `取消提问` in the question bubble's `…` menu (hover, or tap on touch), dismisses with the five-second undo. Question chips and suggested replies never show together; the question wins; both follow the visible project's turn. An answered request keeps its question in the conversation (as the reference recording does at 1:54: the question was asked there, so it stays): where the request sits in the stream, each question is an ordinary Coordinator reply bubble (`QuestionAsked`, `chief/question-answered-line.tsx`: `--reply-bubble-fill`, the question text, its header as the tooltip, `复制` in the hover `…`, no chips), one bubble per question 4px apart in the order asked, joining a Coordinator reply right before it like any reply run. The owner's answer follows 24px under them as one ordinary owner bubble (the `UserMessageBubble` the owner's own messages use, right aligned, joining an owner run after it), holding one line per question in the same order (picked options joined by `、`, or the typed answer). One bubble per request: a multi-question request still sends nothing and shows neither its questions nor an answer in the stream until its last answer. An answered request with nothing in it keeps its question bubbles and the quiet `已回答` line under them; a dismissed question keeps only the quiet `已取消提问` line.
- **Approval card** (`chief/approval-card.tsx`, prototype direction A "the action in one sentence"): every action the owner must approve before it runs (an action-proposal question, `approval-*`, and the `install_packages` request) is one `NoticeBanner` with the `waiting` tone and the action's icon (mail, send, package box, terminal, monitor, else the alert circle), never the raw card text, at most the reply bubble's width. Every kind has the same structure: the title is verb and object in one sentence (`发送邮件给 lena@example.com`, `在 你的 Mac 上以你的身份运行一条命令`, `在 Coordinator 的电脑上安装 ffmpeg 和 libpq-dev`); the body is one `ink-2` line with the account and subject or the sentence itself for a kind the card cannot parse; then the content that will leave or run as a quote block (2px `--border` rule, body ink; commands and package names in mono, one package per line), clamped to 5 lines with `展开全文 · 共 N 行`; then the banner's footer under a hairline: the consequence sentence and the `原始参数` toggle (connector action, `inReplyTo`, `from` in mono on the muted fill) on the left, and the choices on the right in reverse server order, so the approving choice is the rightmost `default` button, a middle choice (`以后都允许`) is `outline` and the declining choice is `ghost`. While the answer is sending every button is disabled and the chosen one carries a spinning `LoaderCircle`. Once answered the card collapses to one `text-meta` history line (`已批准 · 发送邮件给 …`, `已拒绝 · …`, `已安装 · …`, `正在安装 · …` with a spinning icon) with a `详情` link that reopens the full card without buttons; an answered approval adds no owner answer bubble. `NoticeBanner` carries the quote, raw arguments and problem as `children` under the body, and `footer` moves its actions into the footer row; banners without a `footer` keep the one-row layout.

## Live updates stay put (C23)

What the user already sees stays put while a live surface updates. From the first appearance of an element until the turn, task or stream that produced it settles, it is never unmounted and mounted again with the same content, and its offset from the message above it does not change; a reply landing, a refetch or a running flag flipping must not route it through another row on the way. Bug class: a row keyed or grouped by something that changes at settle (a trailing card read as "no reply yet"). Measured by `e2e/chief-live-stability.spec.ts` (remount watcher and offset in `e2e/live-stability.ts`).
