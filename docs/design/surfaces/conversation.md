# Coordinator conversation and home

The `/app` home: the Coordinator's conversation, its composer, status mark, channels and the right-hand panels' shared rules. Reference shots: `docs/design/references/coordinator-chat/` (Claude.ai for replies and the composer, Claude Duet for the shell, floating mark, task references and panels). Everything the Coordinator says sits in its grey bubble in the sans (C31), its one-line handoffs included: `已转给任务 …` reads like any reply. Turn-end markers (`未完成`, `已停止，没有建任务。`, `已停止。停之前已经建了任务 …`) and system notes (who started a merge, which machine a turn ran on) stay status markers in `text-meta` `--text-tertiary`.

There is no floating Coordinator launcher or window: the Coordinator lives only on the home route `/app`. The sidebar's `Coordinator` row is the entry; Cmd/Ctrl+J, notification links and question links navigate to the home, and pending questions raise the row's needs-you count. `apps/web/test/no-floating-coordinator.test.ts` fails if a floating Coordinator returns.

## Shell and top bar

The Coordinator home has a 48px top bar with no visible title (the `Coordinator` or project `h1` is screen-reader only) and the `任务`, `成果` and computer icon buttons on the right; the floating status mark sits top centre.

The status mark (`chief/chief-status-mark.tsx`) is the Coordinator's mascot written straight on the page, with no disc or shadow: one brush line (a filled outline that lands heavy and lifts thin, never textured) at 40px, `foreground` ink, rust for the error cross, 55% ink while offline. Each of the ten states of `statusMarkView` is its own gesture, the brand spiral when idle; a change erases the old line along its path (250ms) and draws the new one (350ms). Idle, done and error hold still; working states re-ink at 5 frames a second; the question bubble carries no dot. The state word sits under it in `text-meta` `--text-secondary` whenever the mark is not idle and changes with the state (something is happening, so there is a word); idle has none, and done's word leaves with done after 2.2 seconds. Hovering adds the turn's elapsed time; the phone shows no word. On the empty home, where the large spiral already stands, the idle mark is hidden; with no model source the empty home shows neither, only the centred connect-a-model-source prompt (a `text-page` lead, a 44px primary button, a `text-ui` hint). At 16 to 24px (the sidebar `Coordinator` row, `CoordinatorGlyph`) the states fold to the brand spiral: tracing while working, the 6px `--waiting-ink` dot while a question waits, an ink cross after an error, dimmed while offline.

The computer button, also the round one on the phone, carries no status dot of its own as in the reference: the Coordinator machine's state is in its tooltip and accessible name, and the fleet badge at the icon's corner carries the machines' online state (see `docs/design/surfaces/computers.md`); it dims only while the connection is down.

The `任务` and `成果` buttons open the right-hand `SidePanel`. `任务` is one list grouped as needs you, in progress and idle (schedules inside idle, shown once and only while their task is not active); `成果` lists finished documents such as research reports, never code changes, which the Tasks panel already shows with their PRs; a card is the title, the report's opening paragraph and its age, and opens the report as a document with its kind, stage and time above a serif title.

Nothing sits above the composer except one notice at a time, in this order: connection lost, `Coordinator 暂时不可用`, the queue-wait card, the failed-turn card, the fallback-model note. The queue-wait card is the same card with an ochre icon for a turn waiting on a computer that is not ready or an offline Runner; it leaves on its own when the turn runs and writes nothing to the conversation. The failed-turn card shows only for the newest failure while no owner message follows it: the outage banner's surface with a rust `CircleAlert`, the title, one sentence, the kind's actions (the first `default`, the rest `outline`), `详情` with the raw error, and a close button; close or Escape hides it, and retry or the next message removes it.

## Coordinator channels

Design `docs/design/coordinator-project-channels.md`. The main channel (`/app`) and each project channel (`/app?project=<id>&chat=1`) are separate Coordinator conversations, each with its own thread, session and turn queue; switching channels switches the thread, never filters one transcript. A channel shows only its own messages; the Coordinator reads another channel with the read-only `channel_messages` tool. Task reports return to the channel that created the task (`create_todo` makes that channel watch it), and a project-channel task that needs the owner or is done also leaves one line in the main channel, carrying the channel it came from.

- **First open of a project channel** creates its thread and one greeting turn: the usual typing state, then a Coordinator bubble of one to three sentences that names the owner, mentions anything in the project waiting on them and asks what to do, with two or three reply chips for that project, from `suggest_replies` during the greeting or from the request that follows it. It runs once per thread. If the owner sends first, the greeting is dropped.
- **Fixed greeting**: when there is nothing to show, the conversation opens with a Coordinator bubble instead of a heading: `{时段}好，{name}。你想让我做什么？` in the main channel, `{name}，你想在 {project} 里做什么？` in a project channel, each with three reply chips `做个计划` / `接一个仓库` / `接着最近的活`. A project channel shows it only when its greeting turn never ran or failed without recovery eligibility, never while the turn's reply is still arriving. A retryable failed initial greeting instead keeps its error visible; only the latest failure offers explicit retry, with the shared plain-language reason and model settings link. Retrying copies the saved greeting prompt without inventing an owner message. No serif heading, date line or counts sit above any conversation; the waiting count is the `任务` badge.
- **Archived project channel**: the transcript as it was, no greeting bubble and no chips.

## Messages and bubbles

- User turns sit on the right in a flat warm sand bubble (`UserMessageBubble`, `data-slot="user-bubble"`): one `--bubble-fill`, no border, no texture and no gradient, `--radius-bubble` (18px) corners and a tail at the bottom right (an 18px-tall flare that leaves the right edge and ends 6px past it on the bubble's baseline, drawn as a clipped `::after` in the bubble's own fill, measured on the reference recording at 1:52), `8px 14px` padding (36px for one line, measured on the reference recording), at most `min(85%, 42rem)` wide.
- Every message is one `MessageBubble` (`components/chat/message-bubble.tsx`) with a side: the owner's on the sand `--bubble-fill`, right-aligned with its tail; the Coordinator's on the grey `--reply-bubble-fill` (#e0dfda light, #252625 dark), left-aligned, no tail. Both are `text-ui` at a 20px line (`CHAT_BODY_CLS`, 14/20) in the sans, as is the composer (owner decision 2026-11-26: one face for everything the Coordinator says); the Coordinator column, composer included, is 756px (`max-w-chat`), measured on the reference recording.
- Coordinator replies carry no avatar column. A reply's paragraphs, headings, blockquote and lists sit in its bubble as one text block; blocks that were separated by a blank line are 12px apart, tighter runs 4px, and the text block, a table and a code block of one reply are 12px apart. Consecutive messages from one author with no divider or other row between them cluster the same way (`chief/bubble-runs.ts`): bubbles of one side sit 2px apart as one group, the corners where two bubbles of a group touch drop to `--radius-sm` (the owner's right side, the Coordinator's left side) while the outer corners keep `--radius-bubble`, and only the last owner bubble of a group carries the tail; this holds at every width and in the task transcript. A centred caption time divider (`今天 15:42`, the later message's time; `components/chat/message-time.tsx` `TimeDivider`) appears only at a day change or a gap over one hour (`lib/message-time.ts` `needsTimeDivider`, used by `chief/time-dividers.ts` `streamDividers`); no hairline and no left bar mark a reply. Tables and code blocks stay in their own faces (the sans and the mono): white surfaces with a 1px `--border` (tables 8px corners, a `secondary` header row, 12px cell padding; code blocks `--radius-code` 10px, a 40px header over a 1px rule: the language label at 12px in `ink-2`, 20px from the left edge, and the expand and copy buttons, 32px at a 32px pitch, each a 16px glyph in full `ink`; measured on the reference, where the glyphs draw 12 to 14.5px of ink in near-black and the label reads #565656). Links in chat text are `--chat-link` (the reference blue, AA on the page and the bubble fills) with a 40% underline in the same hue. Markdown goes through `marked` with GFM and the CJK emphasis extension (`lib/markdown.ts`, the only module that imports it); raw HTML stays literal text and links other than http(s) and cairn's own mention and attachment targets stay literal. `- [ ]`/`- [x]` are read-only checkboxes drawn as cairn's todo glyphs: a 16px dashed `--text-tertiary` circle when open, a filled `--text-tertiary` circle with a check when done, with the done text struck through in `--text-tertiary`; the agent todo card is only for a list carrying the agent's `[~]`/`[/]` progress markers. Headings: H1 18px, H2 and H3 15px, deeper levels body size, all semibold. Every bubble has one meta (`components/chat/message-meta.tsx` `MessageMeta`, which wraps the bubble) beside it on its outer side, centred on the bubble's last line: left of an owner bubble, right of a Coordinator bubble (owner decision 2026-11-26). It holds the time (`MessageTime`: the clock today, the date otherwise, the full date in the title), and `复制` (the whole message), the same on both sides, and on an unread steer a `撤回` icon; the two sides mirror each other, so `复制` sits next to the bubble with the same gap and the time is outermost; there is no more menu and no `选择文字` on desktop (owner decision 2026-11-26). It takes no layout, so hovering moves nothing, and a bubble column leaves room for it; its controls show on hovering the message or focusing into it, and Tab reaches them. Task-transcript agent narration has no bubble, so its meta sits under the text. At 640px or narrower, or on touch, there is no hover meta: a long press (or a right click) on either side opens one menu (`components/chat/message-press-menu.tsx` `MessagePressMenu`) with the time first, then `复制` (the selected part when text in the message is selected, otherwise the whole message) and `选择文字` (selects the message so the system handles can narrow it), and on an unread steer `撤回`; a tap does nothing. A fallback-model note sits under its reply in `--text-tertiary` caption at every width. There is no reaction button and no hover reply button. Quoting starts only from selecting text inside a message (`SelectionReply`). While the Replying to bar is hovered, the quoted message is marked: a reply gets a `--reply-bubble-highlight` wash that reaches 8px past its text without moving it, an owner bubble darkens to `--bubble-highlight`, no ring, and the mark clears as soon as the quote is removed or sent.

## Task references and chips

A task appears in the conversation only inside a sentence (owner: woven into the prose, without a sound; prototype `chat-blend-9`, round 9): every mention of a task, in a reply, a notice, a user bubble or a task transcript, is the task's title as an unframed word (`TaskWord`, `detail/segments.tsx`): `text-ui` medium in the body ink on the sans, no fill, ring or pill, led by a 14px `StatusGlyph` in the state's ink: to start, queued, done, stopped and closed in `--text-tertiary`, planning and building the spinning ring in `--running`, confirm, needs input and review the waiting dot in `--tint-wait-ink`, failed in `--tint-fail-ink`. Hover and focus underline the word on a `--menu-hover` wash; its tooltip and accessible name carry the title, the state in words and what it waits for (`等「…」`). A click opens the task. A title longer than about 22em truncates in the word; reduced motion stops the ring. A mention of a task the page does not know stays the neutral mention chip.

The tasks a turn creates hang on one trunk above its reply bubble (`chief/chief-task-trunk.tsx` `TaskTrunk`, restored 2026-11-27): each branch ends in a 28px task row, the status tile, then the title, and an unread dot when the task's thread has news. Tasks new to a live turn grow on arrival (`chief/task-ref-signal.ts`, siblings 350ms apart: stem, trunk and elbow draw through `stroke-dashoffset`, tile pops in, title characters fade in); when the reply streams an inline `TaskWord` for that task the branch flashes once, and hovering or focusing either the branch or the inline word highlights both. History, reloads, already-present tasks and reduced motion draw at once. Hovering a row opens a popover with the status, what it waits for (`等「X」`), the project and the machine; a click opens the task. One created task is one row of the same trunk; a task created in a turn with no reply stands as an unrooted row (`chief-task-started`), and a task the page no longer knows keeps a plain `#N` link. There is no separate `已建任务` line.

**Naming a task** (C15): a task is named by its title wherever a person reads it — rows, chips, links, status and report lines, confirm dialogs and notifications. A linked mention written as `[#n](todo:<id>)` shows the title as the task word in every renderer. A task the page does not know reads `任务`; a link whose label is not a bare `#n` keeps its author's words. Prose a model wrote as `#n` followed by that task's title (`#6 写作文`, `#6「写作文」`) or as `任务 #n` shows the same word, the title swallowed into it; a bare `#n`, `PR #n` or `owner/repo#n` stays literal. The composer offers no way to insert a task reference: no `#` trigger, no task picker, no inserted token, and the `@` mention list carries no tasks (owner decision 2026-11-22: the user names a task in words; the product never shows `#` for choosing a task); messages already stored with `[#n](todo:<id>)` keep reading by title and linking to the task.

## Composer

- The composer is a one-line raised field (`--composer-fill`: the page surface in light, the reply-bubble grey in dark, as in the reference) with a full 1px inset ring (`--composer-ring`, 13% black light / 7% white dark, not the half-pixel hairline) that strengthens on focus, a soft drop (`--composer-drop`, `0 1px 2px` + `0 3px 10px`), 14px corners and 46px tall at rest, all measured on the reference recording; the thread-panel composer and the narrow one-row composer share the tokens; under it sit the plain `+` menu (attachments, the thread target) and, at the right end, the current context usage: a 14px monochrome ring whose arc is the share of the model window in use, with no number beside it; a short tick on the ring marks the auto-compact point, and within a tenth of it the arc takes `foreground`. The reading in K with English terms (`Context 24K / 200K`, `70K 时 auto-compact` when the server sends a compaction point) is its tooltip and accessible name. The ring never spins: a running turn shows on the status mark and the stop button; while the usage fetch is in flight it is the still bare track. Clicking it opens the context panel (`chief/context-meter-ring.tsx`): `Context` and `76K / 200K`, a bar with the compact tick, `Auto-compact` when the server sends a compaction point, and the channel usage breakdown (`ChannelUsageSection`), with no cache or last-turn hit-rate rows and no manual compact control. It shows whenever context data exists, working or idle. The elapsed time of a running turn shows on hovering the floating status mark. The empty send button is a bare enter icon; once there is a draft the icon turns `foreground` and nothing fills. The stop button is a quiet square with an ink stop glyph.
- **Sending** (first slice of `docs/design/message-first-sending.md`): a message sent to the Coordinator shows at once as a user bubble at 55% opacity with no tools, the composer clears before the server answers, and the bubble turns solid on the answer. A failed send removes the bubble, returns the text to the composer after anything typed since, and shows the `发送失败，请重试。` toast; there is no failed row and nothing is persisted yet.
- **Coordinator spacing** (turn gap re-measured on the reference recording): 24px between a user turn and a reply either way (the reference measures 23 to 24px between bubbles), 20px before a status row or a time divider, on touch too (message tools there appear only on a tap), the status pill at 13px/20px and 34px tall (7px above and below the text, measured on the reference recording at 2:56) under the 44px disc, and `--composer-bottom-gap` 16px under the line below the composer (it was 24px; the task composer shares it).
- **Coordinator message furniture**: the meta beside a message carries its time (`11:20` today, the full date in the title); a day change or a gap of more than one hour puts a centred caption divider (`今天 11:22`) above the next message; its sentences name any task as the task word (glyph and title). Quick-reply chips stay while the turn that offered them is still running (`docs/design/coordinator-quick-replies.md`).
- **Toasts** (reference recording A 1:21-1:30 and 2:48): on desktop every toast stacks at the bottom right, 16px from the right edge, and 112px from the bottom when a composer sits below it (the conversation, a thread panel), 16px when only a side panel is open; below 640px they keep the top-centre placement under the floating controls. A toast is one `popover` card: a 20px glyph in ink (`Info` for success and info; warning and error keep their glyphs and inks; green is never used, V5), the title in regular `text-ui`, the description in `text-meta`, a 28px ghost close button with an `X` at the right of the first line, and the action as a grey 28px `secondary` pill under the text.

## Trace

- The agent's narration stays body text. Tool activity folds into one summary row (`TraceGroupSummary`, `components/chat/trace-parts.tsx`, composed by `TraceGroup`, `TraceRows` and `WorkTrace`). The reference recording has no folded tool line, so the row borrows the rows of its step checklist card (12:28 to 12:56: a 16px state glyph, then the step in grey body type): 28px tall, sized to its content, 12px side padding so its glyph sits on the left edge of the card rows it opens, a 16px `StatusGlyph` in `--text-tertiary` (the filled check when the work is done, the spinning ring while it runs, the `--destructive` cross when a step failed), 8px, one line of `text-ui` 14/20 in `--text-secondary`, 8px, and a 16px `--text-tertiary` chevron that points right when closed and turns down when open, with no transition. Hover fills the whole row with `--menu-hover` at `--radius-control`; nothing is filled at rest, open or closed. The row is the same in the Coordinator conversation and the task thread. The finished line says what came of the group, not the steps it took (step names read as a log; the agent's narration above the group carries the purpose): the files it edited successfully (`改了 rate-limit.ts、login.ts`, first two names then `等 n 个文件`) with the verdict of the last finished test command when it ran after the last edit (`，测试通过` or `，测试未通过`), else that verdict alone, else the files it read (`读了 main.ts`), else the counts ("Ran 2 commands, changed 1 file"). While the group runs the line is the live purpose (the running command's description, or the last reasoning heading), else the present counts. The tooltip holds the purposes the agent gave (reasoning-summary headings and command descriptions, at most two) and the counts. Never hand-write tool rows. The live label (`LiveRow variant="chief"`) stays `text-meta` 13/20.
- Open, the group is a hairline card (1px inset `--border` ring, 12px radius from `--cds-radius-xl`) of 36px rows separated by hairlines. A row reads "verb **target**": the verb in `--text-tertiary`, the target in `--foreground` at medium weight, no kind icon and no code pill; commands are mono without a background. The duration, diff size or exit code sits at the row end in tabular numbers.
- A row with detail opens to a bordered command box (`$ command`, mono `text-code` 13/20, 8px radius, `--background`) with the output under it in grey mono `text-code`.
- Failures: the summary shows the `--destructive` cross and a `--destructive` count ("(1 failed)") and the row a `--destructive` "exit N" or "Failed" label; a failed command opens by default and prints its output in `--destructive`. Never use `--text-dim` for text.
- Running: the summary shows the spinning ring and switches to the present tense for the whole group ("Running 1 command", or the running command's purpose) and sweeps with the `shimmer` utility, as does the verb of the running row. Under reduced motion the shimmer stops.
- `components/ui/trace.tsx` keeps the low-level Trace primitives for thinking rows (`ThinkingRow`).

## Live run

- There is no separate live block: the open group is the live row. In the Coordinator conversation, the transcript ends in the typing bubble (`TypingDots`: three `typing-dot` marks in a filled pill, still under reduced motion); what the Coordinator is doing shows on the status mark, which also carries the queue and quota waits (`排队中`, `等额度`) that used to sit in a line above the composer, and the dots open nothing. A settled reply never lists its tool rows: the Coordinator conversation has no steps view, and calls on the owner's Mac leave no row at all (C26).
- When the turn ends the pulse disappears and the group turns past tense.
- **End space under the Coordinator conversation**: the scroller reaches down to the composer's top edge and reserves `32px` (`pb-8`) below the last message in Coordinator chat (`--chat-end-space` 24px in task transcripts), so a short conversation sits right above the quick-reply chips or the composer (the 160px Claude.ai measurement) and hovering the last message never clips under the composer. The Coordinator stream has no gap above the composer: scrolled, text runs up under the floating header to the window's top edge (the list starts at the top of the pane and pads its first row below the 48px header) and down to the composer's own top edge, so no second line shows. Both edges carry a soft `scroll-fade` like the Claude Duet reference: 32px above the composer and 80px at the top where the list runs under the header or the narrow floating controls (32px below a banner), each off once the list rests at that end. Its scroller reserves the scrollbar gutter on both sides (`scrollbar-gutter-both`), so the message column and the composer share one centre and one width. `FadeScrollerViewport` requires an explicit `reserveEnd`. The task thread transcript reserves only `--chat-end-space`, running or idle, so the newest row sits right above the composer and a running task never leaves a blank band under its live line, as in Claude Code's own panel.

## Questions and approvals

- **Coordinator questions** (C11): a pending question with options is one card in the stream at the point it was asked (`QuestionCard`, `chief/question-card.tsx`, through `PendingQuestion` in `chief/chief-question.tsx`), at most `min(60dvh,560px)` tall with its list scrolling inside: `--radius-composer`, `popover` with a 1px `--border` ring and `--composer-drop` in light, `--composer-fill` with the drop alone in dark. It never blocks and never collapses: the owner keeps writing, the Coordinator keeps replying, and new messages land below it while its controls stay usable in place. The server parks the question as soon as the owner sends a message (or after 70 seconds), so the turn ends and that message is answered; the question stays pending and clickable, while stopping a turn cancels parked non-approval questions. The head holds the question in the reply serif (its header as the title) and, only when the request has more than one step, a `n/N` pager with previous and next. Rows are 40px (44px on touch) with hairline separators: a 24px number key on `secondary` in `--text-tertiary`, the label in `text-ui` ink and the description under it in `text-meta` `--text-tertiary`, always visible. The recommended option is the first row, tagged `推荐` in `--text-tertiary` after its label, and never preselected. A single-select row answers at once (an early step moves to the next step). A multi-select request shows 16px checkboxes and a footer with `已选 N 个`, `跳过` and a round submit, disabled at zero. The last row is the Other input (`输入你自己的回答…`, a pencil key) with `跳过` while empty and a round submit once typed; Enter submits. The composer stays a normal chat composer. The server records a sent message as a card's answer only when it is exactly one option's label (case and outer spaces aside) on exactly one open single-step, single-select card in the same project (any project from the global view), only when it is the owner's first message since the card was asked and no later Coordinator turn has started, never an approval; any other text is only a message and the card stays open. Digits 1 to 9 and the arrow keys move between rows while focus is in the card; in the composer digits are text. The Tasks panel's `待你回答` rows and the needs-you badge scroll to the question. `跳过` skips with the five-second undo. Each card keeps its own draft; a failed answer keeps the card with the error line. The card appears with a 180ms rise only when a question arrives while watching. Question rows and suggested replies never show together; the question wins (C33). A question with no options (one step, not an approval) is no card: it is an ordinary Coordinator bubble with the question text, and the owner's next message in the same project (any project from the global view) is its answer, recorded by the server. Once answered or skipped, a card leaves one compact receipt where it sits in the stream (`QuestionReceipt`, `chief/question-answered-line.tsx`): a `w-fit` box up to the reply width, 14px radius (`rounded-xl`), a 1px `--border` ring (`card` fill in dark), each question in `text-meta` `--text-tertiary` clamped to two lines over its answer in `text-ui` ink (choices joined by `、`), or `已跳过` in `--text-tertiary`. It joins no bubble run. Approvals render their card the same way, in the stream.
- **Approval card** (`chief/approval-card.tsx`, prototype direction A "the action in one sentence"): every action the owner must approve before it runs (an action-proposal question, `approval-*`, and the `install_packages` request) is one `NoticeBanner` with the `waiting` tone and the action's icon (mail, send, package box, terminal, monitor, else the alert circle), never the raw card text, at most the reply bubble's width. Every kind has the same structure: the title is verb and object in one sentence (`发送邮件给 lena@example.com`, `在 你的 Mac 上以你的身份运行一条命令`, `在 Coordinator 的电脑上安装 ffmpeg 和 libpq-dev`); the body is one `ink-2` line with the account and subject or the sentence itself for a kind the card cannot parse; then the content that will leave or run as a quote block (2px `--border` rule, body ink; commands and package names in mono, one package per line), clamped to 5 lines with `展开全文 · 共 N 行`; then the banner's footer under a hairline: the consequence sentence and the `原始参数` toggle (connector action, `inReplyTo`, `from` in mono on the muted fill) on the left, and the choices on the right in reverse server order, so the approving choice is the rightmost `default` button, a middle choice (`以后都允许`) is `outline` and the declining choice is `ghost`. While the answer is sending every button is disabled and the chosen one carries a spinning `LoaderCircle`. Once answered the card collapses to one `text-meta` history line (`已批准 · 发送邮件给 …`, `已拒绝 · …`, `已安装 · …`, `正在安装 · …` with a spinning icon) with a `详情` link that reopens the full card without buttons; an answered approval adds no owner answer bubble. `NoticeBanner` carries the quote, raw arguments and problem as `children` under the body, and `footer` moves its actions into the footer row; banners without a `footer` keep the one-row layout.

## Live updates stay put (C23)

What the user already sees stays put while a live surface updates. From the first appearance of an element until the turn, task or stream that produced it settles, it is never unmounted and mounted again with the same content, and its offset from the message above it does not change; a reply landing, a refetch or a running flag flipping must not route it through another row on the way. Bug class: a row keyed or grouped by something that changes at settle (a trailing card read as "no reply yet"). Measured by `e2e/chief-live-stability.spec.ts` (remount watcher and offset in `e2e/live-stability.ts`).

## Archived project view

The conversation of an archived project keeps its transcript and replaces the composer with one 46px bar at the composer width (the composer surface: `background`, `--radius-composer`, `--shadow-composer`, no border): the `Archive` glyph in `ink-3`, `这个项目已归档。取消归档后才能发消息。` in 14px `ink`, and a secondary `取消归档` button (30px, `secondary` fill, 1px `--border`, `ink` label at weight 500).
