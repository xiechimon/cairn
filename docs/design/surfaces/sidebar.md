# Sidebar and shell

Owner-reviewed sidebar prototype, measured against the Claude Duet recording (1 CSS px = 1.47 frame px). 220px wide on `--sidebar-surface` (the page `sheet`), one `--border` hairline to the main area. Reference shots: `docs/design/references/thread-list-sidebar/`. Source: the owner's reference-versus-ours review (#3144) against Amp and t3code. A list is any group of three or more rows that stand for entities (threads, projects, tasks); measure inside the list container (`aside`, the Tasks panel, a `ListCard`).

## Rules

Scope: `apps/web/src/board/sidebar` `apps/web/src/board/app-sidebar` `apps/web/src/board/archived-project-bar` `apps/web/src/board/archive-toast` `apps/web/src/board/project-` `apps/web/src/board/desktop-update` `apps/web/src/components/shell/` `apps/web/e2e/sidebar` `apps/web/e2e/paper-sidebar` `apps/web/e2e/board-shell` `apps/web/e2e/shell-` `apps/web/e2e/desktop-update` `apps/web/e2e/collapse-family` `apps/web/test/sidebar` `apps/web/test/tasks-sections` `apps/web/test/attention-title`

| L1 | At most 2 distinct hues in a list, the accent included, except the Tasks panel's PR line. | M distinct chromatic hue buckets in the container is <= 2 |
| L2 | Status in a row is a monochrome glyph or a muted text tag; only the state that needs the user may take the accent: a 6px dot, no number. | M `e2e/sidebar-visual.spec.ts`: every project-row glyph color is neutral, the only chromatic mark is the needs-you dot, a status row has `aria-description` |
| L3 | No colored letter or tinted tile for a project or type in a list row; a person or agent may carry its `SeededAvatar`. | M no row descendant outside `[data-slot="avatar"]` has a chromatic background and a one-letter text; `.project-avatar` count is 0 |
| L4 | The title gets the row: at least 80% of a task row's width, or 55% when the row carries meta or menu. | M `title.width / row.width` is >= 0.55 for every sidebar project row |
| L5 | The sidebar is a tree under the Coordinator row: ungrouped projects, group folders, `新建项目`; pinned rows above the footer; archived projects on the flat page. | G `test/sidebar-projects.test.ts`, `test/tasks-sections.test.ts`, `e2e/sidebar-archived.spec.ts`; M header order in the DOM |

| L9 | Row geometry copies the measured reference: 34px rows on a 36px pitch, 14px labels, 22px hover actions, no collapsed rail, a 56px footer. | M `e2e/sidebar-visual.spec.ts`, `e2e/paper-sidebar.spec.ts` |

## Rows and geometry (L9)

Row geometry copies the measured reference: 220px sidebar, 34px rows on a 36px pitch with 8px side insets, 6px corners (`--cds-radius-md`) and 14px `text-ui` labels at weight 400, 26px group rows, 22px hover actions with 16px glyphs (3px clear of the hover fill on every side; 20px glyphs under a coarse pointer, where the action is 44px), no collapsed rail (collapsed is the 28px toggle plus a hover peek, below), and a 56px footer under a hairline rule with 28px ghost icon buttons at the right, 8px edge inset: the desktop update button (desktop app only), usage and settings.

- Usage opens a settings-size dialog: a 7 / 30 / 90 day range in its head; total, Coordinator, tasks and cache hit; the input, output, cache read and cache write bar; tokens per local day as stacked ink bars split by source or by model, starting at the first used day with at least a week shown; then models and projects ranked. There is no per-step split, because task usage before per-step records carries no step kind. The activity list stays in ⌘K search.
- In the Mac desktop shell, where the top row's name sits hidden behind the window controls, the footer's left shows the brand mark and `Cairn` (`sidebar-footer-brand`).
- The update button (`DesktopUpdateButton`) is the circle-arrow-down glyph and its own progress: fill rises with the download percent and is full while installing and restarting; an available update adds a 6px `--waiting-ink` mark, a failure a `--destructive` one; percent and version live only in its tooltip and accessible name.
- The footer has no computers link: the one entry to the computers panel is the laptop button at the top right of the Coordinator home (the round button on the phone), with an 8px status badge at its icon's corner — green all online, red some offline, hollow none online — naming any offline computer in its tooltip and accessible name (`docs/design/surfaces/computers.md`).

## The tree (L5)

The sidebar is a tree under the Coordinator row: unarchived ungrouped projects as its children, then each owner-made group in name order as a folder row with its projects as children (no group row when the owner made none), then `新建项目`. No `项目` label; no task rows; no counts except the project count a folded group shows. Archived projects stay out of the default tree. `新建项目` and `已归档` are pinned above the footer, outside the scroll area; `已归档` opens the archived page in place of the tree. The pinned rows and flat archived rows start in the top-level rows' icon and label columns (icon x 14, label x 42 at 220px).

1. **Brand row**, 48px with a `--border` hairline under it inset 8px on both sides: the collapse toggle (28px button, 22px `PanelLeft` in `ink-3`, centred at 24/24 so it does not move when the sidebar folds), the serif wordmark (Source Serif 4, `text-brand`, weight 500) 3px after it, and search (28px button, 20px icon) at the right edge. Tooltips name `收起侧边栏` and `⌘K`.
2. **Tree**, 8px side insets: `Coordinator` is the root row (icon at 15px, text at 42px). Ungrouped projects hang under it as child rows (row edge at 30px, icon at 40px, text at 68px), then each owner-made group as a 26px folder row with the name and a 12px `ink-3` chevron; a folded group shows `› count` instead. `新建项目` is the last row, indented like a child, without a guide. A group that follows child rows gets 16px more space above it.
3. **Tree guide** before child rows (L8): a 1px `--tree-line` stroke under the parent icon's centre (x 22) that bends with a 4px radius into a horizontal arm at the child icon's centre line and ends at the row's left edge; siblings share one continuous trunk, the last child ends in the elbow, and `新建项目`, `已归档` and the archived page's flat rows have none. The guide is hidden on touch, where rows are 44px. The Coordinator's task rows are not this guide: they keep their tile and rounded elbow, siblings share one `--task-ref-line` trunk with an elbow into each row, and they appear only above the reply whose turn created the task, rising from its bubble, never on one that only mentions it.
4. **Row states**: hover `--sidebar-hover`, selected `--sidebar-active`, project and group labels in `ink` whether selected or not (the reference draws them near-black), the action rows (`新建项目`, `已归档`) and icons in `--sidebar-ink` (`ink-2`), an archived row's label in `ink-2`. On hover or focus a project row reveals a 22px `…` button, a group row its `…` and the Coordinator row the project filter (funnel) and a `+` (new project); the button's pressed or open state is a `--sidebar-press` square on top of the row fill. A project status shows only as the 6px ochre needs-you dot (something waits on the owner, no number); the full status is the row's tooltip and accessible description (L2). A folded group with a waiting project carries the dot. Clicking a project row, in the tree or in the peek, opens that project's channel (`/app?project=<id>&chat=1`, its own Coordinator conversation), as the reference opens a channel; every other entry to a project does the same through `/app/project/<id>` (search rows, the redirect after creating a project, the project settings back link, the not-found suggestions), and the project settings are reached from the project row's `…` menu (`项目设置`; the project's instructions live in its `Memory` tab and its tasks in the Tasks panel, so the menu has no home, tasks or instructions entry); the conversation carries no links row.
5. **Row menu**: at least 168px wide and as wide as its longest label (labels never wrap), 6px padding, 10px corners (`--radius-tile`), `--e2`; items 27px with 6px corners, 20px icons in `--sidebar-ink` 10px from the edge, a 10px icon-to-label gap and 13px labels; each row ends in its menu key (`重命名` R, `项目设置` S, `移到分组` M, `归档` A; group names in `移到分组` have none, as user data), as every action menu does (Menu keys); the hover or open item takes `--sidebar-active`; the divider is inset 10px with 4px above and below. It opens 5px under its trigger, aligned to the trigger's left edge. Project menu: `重命名`, `项目设置`, `移到分组 ›`, `归档`; `项目设置` opens the project's settings. `重命名` and `重命名分组` turn the row itself into a name field (the row's height and glyph, `background` fill, 1px `--focus-ring` inset ring, the name selected): Enter or leaving the field saves, Escape or an empty or unchanged name cancels, the row shows the new name while it saves and goes back with a toast if the save fails; there is no rename dialog. Group menu: `重命名分组`, divider, `解散分组`. Archived project menu: `取消归档` (undo arrow). The UI offers no project deletion (owner decision, 2026-11-21); archive is the only way to put a project away.
6. **Pinned rows and footer**: the sidebar always pins `定时` (`Clock`, opens the Coordinator home with the Tasks panel on its `定时` tab, `--sidebar-active` and `aria-current` while that tab is open there; `/app/schedules` redirects to the same place; a labelled row, not a footer icon, so it is found without hovering) above the footer, and the normal tree adds `新建项目` and `已归档` (archive glyph, always shown, as in the reference) under it, outside the scroll area, on the phone drawer too. Then the 56px footer described above.

## Project filter and the archived page

The Coordinator row's filter (`状态` › `进行中` / `已归档` / `全部`, default `进行中`, kept per device `cairn.sidebarProjectFilter`, read and written in try/catch) shows on the Coordinator row in the tree or in the archived page heading, never both. The funnel opens a row menu with one item `状态` showing the current value in `ink-3` and a submenu `进行中` / `已归档` / `全部` (radio, check on the right; picking one closes the menu). While it is not `进行中` the funnel and `+` stay visible and the funnel carries the `--sidebar-press` fill.

`已归档` opens the archived page inside the sidebar (the same page as the pinned `已归档` entry): the Coordinator row stays, then a hairline rule, then a heading row `已归档的项目` (20px regular `ink`) with a 28px funnel button at its right (16px side inset) (the funnel leaves the Coordinator row meanwhile, so one funnel shows at a time), the `ink-3` 12px note (`已归档的项目和它们的对话会一直保留，取消归档就能放回列表。`, shown only here), a flat list of archived rows without tree guides or group rows, each with a 20px outlined pill (`Archive` 14px glyph + `已归档`, 12px `ink-2`) that the `…` menu replaces on hover and focus (touch shows the menu only), or `没有已归档的项目` when none, and `‹ 进行中` pinned above the footer to switch back, its chevron in the rows' icon column and its label in their label column.

`全部` shows both in tree order and mutes each archived row with the archive tag, no note. An archived row has `ink-2` text, a 22px outlined circle with the 14px `Archive` glyph at the right (hidden under the `…` on hover), the tooltip and accessible description `已归档`, and opens the project's conversation read-only.

## Collapsed and peek

Collapsed leaves no rail (prior art: Sentry's collapsed navigation, Plane's peek sidebar, GitLab's super-sidebar peek). The sheet starts at the window edge with no hairline and only the 28px `展开侧边栏` toggle stays, fixed at the 24/24 centre. Resting the pointer on the toggle or the 8px left screen edge for 250ms floats the whole sidebar over the page as a peek (`--e3`, sliding in over `--dur-fast`, no layout shift), so Coordinator, every project and its needs-you dot read at a glance; the toggle stays on top of the peek with its `展开侧边栏` tooltip and the peek's own toggle is hidden. Leaving for 300ms tucks it away unless a row menu is open or keyboard focus is inside. Keyboard focus on the toggle opens the peek and Tab moves into it, Escape closes it and returns focus to the toggle, Enter or a click expands for good. Touch never peeks: a tap expands. Rows in the peek navigate as in the docked sidebar and the sidebar stays collapsed. Collapsing by hand does not peek until the pointer has left. On a docked desktop layout, opening a side panel (tasks, results, computers, a file, a task thread) collapses the sidebar the same way; expanding while the panel is open sticks, and closing the panel restores the owner's own choice. Below 640px the drawer stays as it is.

## Shell parts

`components/shell/`: `SidebarNavRow` (its global key, `C` or `⌘K`, always shows on the right as a quiet key hint), `SidebarProjectRow` (a child row with its tree guide, neutral glyph, no count, an ochre dot when a task waits on the owner, status in the tooltip), `SidebarProjectGroupHeader` (an owner-made group folder row that folds to a count), `SidebarUser` (no status dot), `WaitPill`, `HeaderIconButton` with attention dot, `ProjectCard`, `PhoneTabBar` with badge.

The main surface is flat and edge to edge: the `background` sheet fills the window beside the sidebar, separated from the sidebar (which shares the sheet surface) by one 1px `--border` hairline on the sheet's left edge, with no margin, corner or shadow. The desktop thread panel sits beside the conversation with a 1px `--border` left divider and no shadow. At 640px and below the hairline goes away. The new-task row uses a filled `--card-button` circle for its plus icon and shows its `C` key as the same quiet key hint as the search row's `⌘K`.
