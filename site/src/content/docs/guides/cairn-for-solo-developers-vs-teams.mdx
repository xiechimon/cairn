Cairn is designed strictly for **one person**, not a multi-user engineering team. Each person who wants Cairn runs their own Mac desktop app (or self-hosted server), connects their own model subscriptions, and owns all data on their own hardware.

## What Cairn intentionally leaves out

Because Cairn focuses on the solo developer workflow—one Coordinator supervising background work across your own machines and phone—several multiplayer features are explicitly out of scope:

- **No shared chat spaces or multi-user permissions:** Only the owner (and paired devices belonging to that owner) accesses the instance.
- **No pooled team billing or shared subscription seats:** Every user supplies their own ChatGPT, Claude Code, or API credentials.
- **No per-seat admin dashboards:** Settings like **Team secrets** in the UI refer to the single personal tenant boundary inside your own database, not a multi-user org.

## When to pick a team tool instead

If your primary requirement is a shared workspace where multiple engineers watch the same agent threads, manage role permissions, and share one company billing pool, use a multiplayer product such as [Amp Spaces](https://ampcode.com/docs/spaces) ([Cairn vs Amp Spaces](/guides/cairn-vs-amp-spaces/)). For solo developers who want full ownership on their Mac and personal devices, Cairn avoids multiplayer complexity completely.
