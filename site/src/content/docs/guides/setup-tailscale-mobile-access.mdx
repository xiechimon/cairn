Tailscale Serve lets you open your Mac or home-server Cairn instance from your iPhone or Android device over private HTTPS without exposing any port to the public internet. Tailscale handles encrypted tailnet transport while Cairn still enforces single-use device pairing codes and `HttpOnly` session cookies.

## Step 1: Prepare your server host and Tailscale

1. Install and sign in to Tailscale on both your Cairn host computer and your phone.
2. In the Tailscale admin console, enable **MagicDNS** and **HTTPS certificates** under **DNS**.
3. Check the running loopback port (the Cairn Mac desktop app chooses a free port displayed in its menu bar menu; standalone `npx @xiechimon/cairn` defaults to `8787`).

## Step 2: Start Tailscale Serve (never Funnel)

On the host running Cairn (replace `<port>` with the menu bar port on Mac desktop or `8787` on `npx`):

```sh
tailscale status
tailscale serve --bg http://127.0.0.1:<port>
tailscale serve status
```

If Serve prints an enablement URL, visit it once to enable Serve for your tailnet, then run the command again. Copy the printed HTTPS hostname, such as `https://<machine>.<tailnet>.ts.net`. Do **not** run `tailscale funnel`, which publishes your endpoint to the public internet, and avoid `tailscale serve reset` if you have other Serve routes.

## Step 3: Pair your phone and enable Web Push

1. On your Cairn desktop owner session, open **Settings -> Remote access** and mint a one-time pairing code.
2. Open `https://<machine>.<tailnet>.ts.net/app` on your phone and enter the code.
3. On iPhone, use Safari's **Add to Home Screen** before turning on push notifications so iOS Web Push can deliver approval alerts.

Note: a lost paired phone can still approve Runners until revoked; remove it immediately from **Settings -> Remote access** on your owner machine if lost.
