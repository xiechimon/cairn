An always-on Mac mini is the ideal home for Cairn because the Mac desktop app bundles the server, local pi Runner, and owner session in one menu-bar process without draining your laptop battery. Closing the desktop window hides the UI while keeping the background server and Runner alive; quitting the app stops both cleanly.

## Why developers pair a Mac mini with Cairn

- **Native macOS toolchain:** Xcode, Apple Silicon builds, local databases, and macOS Seatbelt task confinement work natively without Linux container workarounds.
- **Background work while you step away:** The Coordinator runs research, builds, and tests on the Mac mini while you travel with an iPhone or MacBook.
- **No public ports:** Putting `tailscale serve` in front of Cairn's loopback port gives your phone private HTTPS access on your tailnet—with one-time device pairing codes still required—instead of exposing your server to the public internet.

## Recommended Mac mini checklist

1. Install `Cairn-<version>-mac-arm64.dmg` in `/Applications` and clear the unnotarized Gatekeeper prompt once ([Gatekeeper guide](/guides/macos-gatekeeper-blocks-cairn-first-launch/)).
2. Enable **Prevent automatic sleeping when the display is off** in macOS Energy settings so long-running builds do not pause mid-step.
3. Configure [Tailscale Serve](/guides/setup-tailscale-mobile-access/) for mobile web access, or connect [Telegram or Slack](/guides/connect-telegram-slack-im-notifications/) for instant approval cards in chat.
4. Back up `~/Library/Application Support/@cairn/desktop/` (keeping both `server.db` and `secretbox.key` together).
