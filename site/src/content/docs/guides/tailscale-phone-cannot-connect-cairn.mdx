When your iPhone cannot open Cairn or pairing fails over Tailscale, the culprit is almost always one of four settings: MagicDNS/HTTPS not enabled, `tailscale serve` pointing at the wrong loopback port, an iOS VPN conflict blocking `100.64.0.0/10`, or an expired/used pairing code.

## Diagnostic checklist (in order)

1. **Confirm `tailscale serve status` points to your active Cairn port:**
   ```sh
   tailscale serve status
   ```
   Remember that the Cairn Mac desktop app picks a free loopback port (shown in its menu bar menu), whereas `npx @xiechimon/cairn` defaults to `127.0.0.1:8787`. Make sure `tailscale serve` proxies the exact running port.
2. **Use the HTTPS MagicDNS hostname, never a raw IP:** Open `https://<machine>.<tailnet>.ts.net/app` in Safari. Both MagicDNS and HTTPS certificates must be enabled in the Tailscale admin console.
3. **Check iOS VPN conflicts:** iOS allows only one active VPN-class tunnel at a time. If you use a proxy or TUN-mode VPN alongside Tailscale, route `100.64.0.0/10` directly or disable the conflicting tunnel.
4. **Mint a fresh pairing code:** Pairing codes are single-use and time-limited. Generate a new code from your desktop owner session under **Settings -> Remote access**.

Also confirm your Mac did not go to sleep—if the server machine sleeps, the tailnet HTTPS page stops responding until it wakes ([Tailscale setup guide](/guides/setup-tailscale-mobile-access/)).
