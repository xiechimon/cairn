**New measured pitfalls**: (1) `useProjects` hits `/api/projects?teamId=` — the
projects stub pattern in stubBoot must carry `?*`, a bare `**/api/projects` does not
match URLs with a query; the symptom is the whole page gets no project rows and file

probe.
