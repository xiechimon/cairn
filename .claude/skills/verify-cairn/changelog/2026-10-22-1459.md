Custom probe `scripts/drive-1137-webhook-tracer.mjs` (case `github-webhook/webhook-tracer`) catches up with #1459: the webhook's `pacman` trigger, since #1477, checks the labeler per ADR 0021 D5 (the event's `sender` needs triage or above on the repo), counts toward the project's in-flight cap (3), and refuses everything with a flat 202. After #1477 merged, the probe's deliveries carried no `sender` and every one was refused as no-sender, A1 onward all red; #1496 fixed it.

- New env `VERIFY_GITHUB_SENDER`: the sender of the delivery, must be a login with triage, write, maintain or admin on `VERIFY_GITHUB_REPO`, and `GH_WEBHOOK_VERIFY_TOKEN` must be able to read its permission. Defaults to the owner segment of `VERIFY_GITHUB_REPO` — a personal repo's owner is an admin, an org repo must be given explicitly (an org login is not a user, the permission read 404s = below triage, A1 goes red). Registered in the case's `usage` in `vp-registry.json`.
- Egress goes from "one call" to three kinds of real reads: issue details, labels, and the labeler's permission (once per new sender).
- New checks: K1 payload without sender -> 202 ignored "event has no sender"; K2 sender = `ghost` (GitHub's placeholder account when it cannot recognize a person, permission read 404s) -> 202 ignored, with the detail line shared by all labeler-permission refusals (it does not say which kind); K3 directly insert two in-flight github-issue cards to fill the cap of 3 -> 202 ignored up to the cap; K4 none of the three refusal kinds creates a card. L1-L3 find the reason codes no-sender / sender-below-triage / at-cap in server.log by deliveryId (written in two shapes: pretty form has the fields a few lines after the deliveryId, JSON form has them on the same line — not yet verified against a live stack); the evidence slice `11-tracer-server-log.txt`'s line window widens from +/- 1 to -1..+8 so the reason-code lines are captured; J1 changes to requiring each of the 8 consumed deliveries to have a deliveryId line in server.log (previously it only counted total lines and one unrecorded delivery could still pass).
