# 2026-10-30 · Optional evidence archives

Issue #2200 makes repository evidence archives optional. Verification reports still name the command, result, provider mode, and remaining limits. Supplied images must be embedded and reachable; they need not be committed under `docs/verify/`.

The archive CLI remains available and preserves existing artifacts. Historical changelog entries describe the policy in effect at their dates. The implementation checks and prepush plan comparison for this change are under `docs/verify/2200/`.
